I parked at the south lot at 6:40 a.m. and the coffee was already burnt. Priya had printed the routing sheet on yellow paper, the kind that smears if you rest your thumb on it.

We missed the 7:15 window by nine minutes. I still think the nine minutes were the garage gate, not the loader. The loader, Ken, had been there since five.

Hale paid £18.40 an hour then. Nobody wrote a think-piece about it. We went back out.
