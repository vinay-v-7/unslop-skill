This paper sits at the intersection of transport geography and labour studies. Why night-bus reliability matters cannot be overstated. We utilise administrative ticketing data (TfL, 2024) and a driver survey (n = 118) to argue that headway variance is associated with missed rest, highlighting a broader crisis.

Results suggest a 6.2 min increase in P95 headway on the N5 after 02:00 (95% CI 4.1–8.3). Causal claims should be treated as provisional rather than definitive. As of my last knowledge cutoff, further work will explore wellbeing.

Keywords: night buses; headway; labour
