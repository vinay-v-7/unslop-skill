# Mode: short-form

Load when: platform post, caption, thread, hook line, one-line paragraphs, hashtags. Token: `short`.
Default tier: strict
Default volume: free
Structural phases: none

## 1 What good human writing looks like here

A post only this person could have made. One claim, one number or named thing, one ask or none. Paragraphs may be one line. Lowercase starts are native, not errors.

## 2 Tells that matter most

Ranked; patterns in [lexicon-2026.md](../references/lexicon-2026.md), habits in [habits.md](../references/habits.md).

1. Reveal bridges (`here's what`, `the result?`) — structure family
2. "Here's the thing" / "let that sink in" / sincerity openers
3. Staccato stacks and fragment runs (RHYTHM, [surface.md](../references/surface.md))
4. Closing-question bait ("What do you think?", "Agree?", "Tag someone")
5. Negative parallelism as the whole post
6. Significance tics (`this matters`) on a 200-word caption

## 3 Not tells here (protect)

- One-line paragraphs and blank lines between them
- Lowercase sentence starts
- One fragment, one triad, one dash
- Hashtags the author wrote
- Direct "you"

## 4 Structural work

None. Spine only: one sentence + claims that must survive. Do not outline, do not add a hook formula.

## 5 Voice moves

- Required (from author material only): one specific, one opinion with a stake
- Allowed: one-line layout, informal spelling, a joke
- Forbidden: new hooks, new closing questions, sincerity frames, stock slack, invented anecdotes

## 6 Questions to ask the author

At most three, [interview.md](../references/interview.md). Prefer: the number with a date; the named person or tool; what they would not repeat.

## 7 Length and volume

Usually ≤~600 words. Longer: reread as essay or article and say so. Volume free: cut filler; length may shrink.

## 8 Mode final check

Could only this person have posted this? If the first sentence could go to a hundred people unchanged, it is still a template.
