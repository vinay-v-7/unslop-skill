# Mode: fiction

Load when: scenes, characters, dialogue, POV; or verse. Tokens: `fiction`, `poetry`.
Default tier: strict on **narration only**
Default volume: careful
Structural phases: STORYSCOPE A→B→C at ≥1,500 words ([structural.md](../references/structural.md), [fingerprints.md](../references/fingerprints.md) §1). Under that: CUT/RHYTHM on narration, leave plot.

## 1 What good human writing looks like here

People talk like people. Weather can be weather. A character can be angry in those words. Endings need not "understand and accept". The lexicon is a narration tool, not a dialogue scrubber.

## 2 Tells that matter most

On **narration** only:

1. Fingerprints §1: flat escalation, epilogue coda, default uncanny mood, systematic no-dreams
2. Mirrored weather / setting-as-psyche
3. Reflexive embodied emotion (tight chest, dry throat) on every beat
4. "Understands and accepts" endings
5. Thematic asides from a narrator who steps outside every character
6. Lexicon significance and corrective-framing tics in exposition

Dialogue is not scored.

## 3 Not tells here (protect)

- Dialogue, including fragments, swearing, repetition, ungrammatical speech
- Explicit emotion labels
- One-sentence paragraphs used as beats
- Author fragments and run-ons that are voice
- Verse line breaks

## 4 Structural work

≥1,500 words: Phase A pick 3–4 cluster constraints; B draft holding them; C shortlist the 3 worst cluster offenders — wording cannot close a Phase C finding. Prefer subtraction on AI-elevated axes. Thin epilogues.

### Poetry

Heart / echo / whisper as the load-bearing move, not those words. Default stanza: quatrain unless the source is otherwise. Do not regularize meter the author broke. Do not insert rhyme. Lineation stays.

## 5 Voice moves

- Required: POV, tense, and names as given
- Allowed: in-dialogue character intro; one irrational beat if the story wants it
- Forbidden: new characters or events in someone else's fiction (propose, do not insert); stocking "humanizing" sensory lists; scrubbing dialogue against the lexicon

## 6 Questions to ask the author

What must not change in the plot? Whose POV is this? For poetry: keep lineation, or may reflow?

## 7 Length and volume

Any length. Careful: 80–110% on narration. Dialogue byte-stable unless they ask.

## 8 Mode final check

Dialogue untouched by the lexicon. No new plot. Fingerprints §1 if long enough. Character floor: named specifics still in.
