# Mode: business

Load when: salutation or sign-off, subject line, memo, DM, cover letter, proposal. Tokens: `business`, `email`.
Default tier: strict
Default volume: free
Structural phases: none

## 1 What good human writing looks like here

Why you're writing, the ask, the date, the number. Then stop. Warmth the author already had may stay. Performed warmth may not.

## 2 Tells that matter most

1. "Hope this finds you well" / "I hope this email finds you"
2. Sycophantic openers ("Excited to connect", "I was impressed by your incredible…")
3. Warm closers ("Don't hesitate to reach out", "Please do not hesitate")
4. Uniform 30–50 word paragraphs, every one
5. Length inflation: three paragraphs of context before the ask
6. "Circling back" / "Just wanted to follow up" with no new fact
7. Promotional verbs in a proposal ("leverage", "unlock", "robust")

## 3 Not tells here (protect)

- The ask, deadline, and every number — **byte-identical**
- Names, subject line intent, legal or pricing clauses
- The author's real sign-off name and title
- Short DMs
- Formal "Dear" if they used it

## 4 Structural work

None. Lead with the reason. One ask. If there are two asks, say so; do not bury the second.

## 5 Voice moves

- Required: ask, deadline, numbers, names unchanged
- Allowed: one human sentence they already wrote
- Forbidden: new small-talk, new compliments, new hedges, "just circling back" if they didn't write it, invented availability

## 6 Questions to ask the author

What is the ask in one line? Deadline and timezone? Number they will be held to?

## 7 Length and volume

Usually ≤600 words. Free: cut throat-clearing. Cover letters: careful, keep structure.

## 8 Mode final check

Ask, deadline, numbers still byte-identical. Opener is the reason. No sycophancy you added.
