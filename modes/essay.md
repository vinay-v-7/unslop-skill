# Mode: essay

Load when: first-person argued thesis or reflection, one idea with a turn. Token: `essay`.
Default tier: strict
Default volume: careful if author-written; free if AI-drafted
Structural phases: none under ~2,500 words; above that, treat as longform and say so

## 1 What good human writing looks like here

One idea, a turn, a mind changing on the page. Digression that earns its keep. "I think" is load-bearing. Jagged attention is allowed. The outline of first sentences should not read like a table of contents.

## 2 Tells that matter most

1. "this matters" / "why X matters" (Opus 5.x, lexicon)
2. "is more than X, it's a Y" / "more than just"
3. Orphaned "This highlights…" / "This means…" ([habits.md](../references/habits.md) §1)
4. Aphorism closers on every paragraph (clean slop)
5. Outline test: tidy first-sentence summary
6. Corrective framing ("not simply", "rather than relying")
7. Inflated significance without a fact

## 3 Not tells here (protect)

- "I think", "I don't know", "I was wrong"
- Digressions, parenthetical asides, self-interruption
- Uneven paragraph length
- A real unanswered question
- Honest hedges on the actual soft spot

## 4 Structural work

None unless length crosses into longform. Keep the turn. Do not add a recap ending.

## 5 Voice moves

- Required: the author's claim and any first-person already present
- Allowed: one punchy close in the whole essay, not per paragraph
- Forbidden: new sincerity, invented examples, stock slack, synonym swaps from the lexicon

## 6 Questions to ask the author

The missing fact that would make the turn land; the counterargument they actually hold; what they no longer believe.

## 7 Length and volume

600–2,500 words typical. Careful: structure survives, length 80–110%. Free (AI): delete restatement.

## 8 Mode final check

Does the turn still happen? Are first sentences a tidy outline? If yes, break one. Character floor held?
