# Mode: longform-nonfiction

Load when: nonfiction ≥~2,500 words or chaptered — feature, report narrative, book chapter. Token: `longform`.
Default tier: strict
Default volume: careful
Structural phases: yes — high-confidence rows of [nonfiction.md](../references/nonfiction.md) **before** wording. [structural.md](../references/structural.md) is fiction; do not apply it here.

## 1 What good human writing looks like here

A through-line and at least one complication. Unresolved endings are allowed. A digression that does not serve the thesis can stay. Named specifics outrank atmosphere.

## 2 Tells that matter most

1. Thematic over-determination: lesson sentences, "what this means"
2. Narratorial meta: "it's worth noting", "this is a crucial distinction"
3. Intertextual vagueness: "research suggests" with no name
4. Tidy closure / motivational bow
5. Corrective framing and significance tics (lexicon §Opus / Astra)
6. Machine-flat stretches of equal sentence length
7. Outline test across H2s

Use **high-confidence** nonfiction rows only unless asked. Low-confidence rows (anachrony, location tour) are speculative.

## 3 Not tells here (protect)

- Unresolved endings
- A genuine second thread (counterargument, parallel case)
- Long sentences that carry a real subordinate clause
- The author's reported speech and notebooks
- Section breaks the author chose

## 4 Structural work

Restructuring comes before wording. Pick 3–4 live constraints from high-confidence rows. Prefer subtraction on AI-elevated axes (restated lessons, meta-commentary, tidy bows). Move toward the analogue of the human mean and stop. After structure settles, CUT / RHYTHM / ADD.

## 5 Voice moves

- Required: names, numbers, scenes from source; the author's position
- Allowed: one direct-address aside; one open question at the end
- Forbidden: invented color, composite anecdotes, stock slack, new hooks

## 6 Questions to ask the author

The named study or person; the cost of the recommendation; what remains unknown.

## 7 Length and volume

≥~2,500 words. Careful: 80–110%. Do not shrink a chapter into a blog post.

## 8 Mode final check

Structure before wording was actually done. Unresolved ending survived if it was there. High-confidence rows only, unless asked. Character floor held across sections.
