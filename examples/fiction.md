# Example: fiction narration

Mode `fiction`, AI-drafted, strict on narration, careful. Dialogue is left alone. The epilogue coda is the STORYSCOPE tell.

## Before (excerpt)

The rain understood her grief. Mara's chest tightened as she approached the cottage, each drop a reminder of the life she had lost. She understood, at last, and accepted that the past would always be with her.

"You coming in or you going to drown?" said Len from the stoop, cigarette already dying.

"Give me a minute," she said. "This weather is a lot."

Looking ahead, the years would be quieter. In the epilogue of her life she would keep the kettle on the back burner and think of him whenever the tin roof ticked. This matters, she told herself. The cottage was more than a house, it was a wound that had learned to be a home.

## After

Rain on the cottage roof. Mara walked the last fifty yards of ruts and stopped at the gate, which still sat one hinge low, same as June.

"You coming in or you going to drown?" said Len from the stoop, cigarette already dying.

"Give me a minute," she said. "This weather is a lot."

She was angry, and she did not tidy it. Len did not ask. The kettle was already on the back burner. The hinge could wait until morning.

Narration lost the mirrored weather, the embodied chest, the "understands and accepts" close, the fake epilogue, and the lexicon tics. Dialogue, including "This weather is a lot.", stayed.
